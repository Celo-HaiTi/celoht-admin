# Celo-HaiTi Governance

Celo-HaiTi's Governance Council is its highest ongoing collective decision-making body. Governance authority is collective and must not be inferred from a person's founding status, operational role, technical maintenance responsibilities, or partnership representation.

## Roles and boundaries

- **Governance Council:** The highest ongoing collective decision-making body for Celo-HaiTi's governance matters.
- **Founder:** The Founder role records Celo-HaiTi's history and may include founding, representative, or strategic responsibilities. It does not by itself confer governance authority, veto power, or unilateral control.
- **Maintainers:** Maintainers provide technical and project stewardship within their documented scope. Maintenance responsibility does not place Maintainers above the Governance Council.
- **Working Groups:** Working Groups carry out delegated or otherwise scoped work within their documented mandates. A Working Group's operational remit does not make it a higher governance body.
- **Contributors:** Contributors participate in the project through the contribution and governance framework, including proposing work and contributing to community activity as applicable.
- **Partnership and operations:** Partnership, representation, and operational responsibilities are carried out within Celo-HaiTi's governance and operational framework. They do not, by themselves, create governance authority.

This document does not create additional voting, quorum, approval, veto, election, term, treasury, or execution rules. Those mechanics are authoritative only where separately documented by Celo-HaiTi's approved governance materials.
